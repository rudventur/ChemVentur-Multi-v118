## Left panel — "folded" version

**Status:** Not started. Today the left panel only fully shows or fully hides
(toggle button at the top-left corner, next to the logo when the panel is
open). That fixed the mobile bug where the panel covered the entire screen
with no way to reach the game canvas underneath it.

**What's planned:** a third state between "open" and "hidden" — a folded
strip (icon-only buttons, no labels/text) docked at the left edge, so the
most-used controls (gun selector, stage select, connect) stay reachable
without covering the canvas the way the full panel does. Mainly aimed at
phones, but should work on desktop too.

Not scoped yet — needs a decision on which controls make the cut for the
folded strip vs. staying panel-only.
